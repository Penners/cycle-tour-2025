# 🚲 Tour Journal: Normandy to Brittany (August 31 – September 11, 2025)

## 📌 Tour Overview
- **Route:** Bristol ➔ Portsmouth (Train) ➔ Ouistreham (Overnight Ferry) ➔ the D-Day beaches ➔ Carentan ➔ Avranches (Train) ➔ Mont-Saint-Michel ➔ Saint-Malo ➔ Dinard (Ferry) ➔ the Côte d'Émeraude ➔ Saint-Brieuc ➔ Paimpol ➔ the Pink Granite Coast ➔ Morlaix ➔ Roscoff ➔ Plymouth (Ferry) ➔ Bristol (Train)
- **Riders:** Penry & Mike
- **Style:** Campsite touring, mostly municipal sites, with one train shortcut and three ferries
- **Distance:** 541 km ridden over 11 riding days, plus 115 km by train in France
- **Conditions:** Changeable late-summer weather: mostly high teens to low 20s °C, with a blustery south-westerly that gusted to 70+ km/h in Normandy, a warm, still spell on the Côte d'Émeraude, and grey, showery days into Finistère.

---

## 🗓 Day-by-Day Chronicle

### **Sunday, 31 August 2025**
* **Route:** Bristol ➔ Portsmouth Harbour (Train) ➔ Portsmouth International Port ➔ Ouistreham (Overnight Ferry)
* **Weather:** Mostly clear, about **19°C**, with a breezy south-westerly.
* **Log:** We hung the bikes up on the train out of Bristol in the afternoon and had a pint in Portsmouth before riding down to the ferry port, where we queued with the other cyclists and wheeled the bikes onto the car deck just after 11 PM. The Spinnaker Tower was lit up red as we sailed out of Portsmouth Harbour.
* **Overnight:** The Brittany Ferries night crossing to Ouistreham.

---

### **Monday, 1 September 2025**
* **Route:** Ouistreham ➔ Lion-sur-Mer ➔ Saint-Aubin-sur-Mer ➔ Bernières-sur-Mer ➔ Courseulles-sur-Mer ➔ Graye-sur-Mer ➔ Ver-sur-Mer ➔ Arromanches ➔ Tracy-sur-Mer (36 km)
* **Weather:** Sunny morning, about **20°C**; a south-westerly picked up through the day and rain set in by the afternoon (7 mm). Overnight low **12°C**.
* **Log:** We docked at Ouistreham at dawn and were riding along Sword Beach by 7:30 AM, with the sun rising over the sea. From there we followed the D-Day coast west: the Churchill AVRE tank at Lion-sur-Mer, the Allied flags at Bernières (Juno Beach), the "Bold" Sherman and the guns at Courseulles, and the Cross of Lorraine at Graye-sur-Mer, where we stopped to fix a puncture. We reached Arromanches before 11 AM for the view over the Mulberry harbour from the cliff top, then a short hop on to the campsite.
* **Overnight:** Camping Les Bas Carreaux (Tracy-sur-Mer, near Bayeux). The tent went up in the rain.

---

### **Tuesday, 2 September 2025**
* **Route:** Tracy-sur-Mer ➔ Longues-sur-Mer ➔ Colleville-sur-Mer (Omaha Beach) ➔ Saint-Laurent-sur-Mer ➔ Vierville-sur-Mer ➔ Grandcamp-Maisy ➔ Les Veys ➔ Carentan (72 km)
* **Weather:** Sunny spells (6½ hours of sunshine) but blustery: a **31 km/h** south-westerly gusting to **71 km/h**, in our faces for nearly two-thirds of the ride. About **20°C**, with showers (7 mm).
* **Log:** Out along the farm lanes to the Longues-sur-Mer battery, where the original guns still sit in their casemates. From there to Omaha Beach: the 1st Infantry Division monument, the Normandy American Cemetery at Colleville, and "Les Braves" on the sand at Saint-Laurent, then the D-Day Omaha museum at Vierville and on through Grandcamp-Maisy. The longest day of the trip, 72 km, finishing in Carentan just after 7 PM.
* **Overnight:** Flower Camping Le HautDick (Carentan), the first of two nights.

---

### **Wednesday, 3 September 2025**
* **Route:** Carentan ➔ Saint-Côme-du-Mont ➔ Sainte-Mère-Église ➔ Saint-Martin-de-Varreville ➔ Utah Beach ➔ Sainte-Marie-du-Mont ➔ Carentan (57 km)
* **Weather:** The wildest day: a **36 km/h** south-westerly gusting to **74 km/h**, with heavy showers (8 mm) between bright spells. About **20°C**.
* **Log:** A day-loop from the campsite. We sat out a shower before setting off after 1:30 PM, past the battered Sherman at Saint-Côme-du-Mont, to the Airborne Museum at Sainte-Mère-Église, with its C-47 and Waco glider, and the church with the John Steele paratrooper still hanging from the tower. We waited out another downpour at Saint-Martin-de-Varreville before the Leclerc monument and the bunkers in the dunes at Utah Beach, then the Utah Beach Museum and Sainte-Marie-du-Mont's church in the evening sun on the way back.
* **Overnight:** Flower Camping Le HautDick (Carentan).

---

### **Thursday, 4 September 2025**
* **Route:** Carentan ➔ Avranches (Train, via Saint-Lô and Coutances) ➔ Céaux ➔ Mont-Saint-Michel ➔ Ardevon ➔ Saint-Georges-de-Gréhaigne ➔ Cherrueix (54 km)
* **Weather:** About **20°C** with a westerly wind, in our faces for most of the ride, and a shower or two (5 mm). Cooler night, down to **11°C**.
* **Log:** A morning wander round Carentan's square and medieval arcades before the 11:15 train south. We started riding from Avranches at about 1:15 PM, catching our first glimpse of Mont-Saint-Michel from the cycle path at Courtils, then crossed the salt meadows and the footbridge right up to the foot of the Mont around 3 PM, before following the bay west into Brittany.
* **Overnight:** Le Tenzor de la Baie (Cherrueix).

---

### **Friday, 5 September 2025**
* **Route:** Cherrueix ➔ Saint-Méloir-des-Ondes ➔ Cancale ➔ Saint-Coulomb ➔ Saint-Malo ➔ Dinard (Ferry) ➔ Lancieux ➔ Saint-Jacut-de-la-Mer (55 km)
* **Weather:** Calm and dry at last: light winds, about **19°C**.
* **Log:** Along the Bay of Mont-Saint-Michel past the mussel farmers' boats on wheels at Cherrueix and an oyster stall at Saint-Méloir-des-Ondes, to Cancale at low tide. In Saint-Malo by about 1:30 PM for a wander inside the walls and the tidal pool at Plage de Bon-Secours, then the bus de mer across the Rance to Dinard mid-afternoon and on along the coast.
* **Overnight:** Camping Municipal de la Manchette (Saint-Jacut-de-la-Mer).

---

### **Saturday, 6 September 2025**
* **Route:** Saint-Jacut-de-la-Mer ➔ Matignon ➔ Plurien ➔ Pléneuf ➔ Morieux ➔ Viaduc des Ponts-Neufs ➔ Saint-Brieuc (68 km)
* **Weather:** The warmest day: about **25°C**, dry, with a south-easterly behind us. A mild night, about **16°C**.
* **Log:** The hilliest day of the trip (about 800 m of climbing) along the Côte d'Émeraude. We crossed the old Viaduc des Ponts-Neufs on the cycle path in the afternoon and reached Saint-Brieuc around 5 PM, then went into town for a beer in the evening.
* **Overnight:** Camping des Vallées (Saint-Brieuc).

---

### **Sunday, 7 September 2025**
* **Route:** Saint-Brieuc ➔ Pordic ➔ Saint-Quay-Portrieux ➔ Plouha ➔ Beauport ➔ Paimpol (49 km)
* **Weather:** Grey and showery (9 mm) but warm, about **23°C**, with a gusty southerly mostly behind us.
* **Log:** North up the Goëlo coast, with the trip's highest point (about 127 m) on the way. We passed the pond at Beauport into Paimpol, arriving around 2:30 PM.
* **Overnight:** Accueil Camping (Paimpol).

---

### **Monday, 8 September 2025**
* **Route:** Paimpol ➔ Pleumeur-Gautier ➔ Plouguiel ➔ Louannec (40 km)
* **Weather:** Sunny spells, about **19°C**, but a westerly in our faces for most of the ride.
* **Log:** A stop at La Maison de la Sardine in Paimpol before setting off late morning. We crossed the Tréguier estuary and reached the campsite at Louannec by about 2:15 PM. Mid-afternoon we went into Louannec village, to the church and Bar Le Village. Later we walked about 100 metres from our pitch to the beach right next to the campsite and swam in the sea.
* **Overnight:** Camping Municipal Ernest Renan (Louannec).

---

### **Tuesday, 9 September 2025**
* **Route:** Louannec ➔ Lannion (Brélévenez) ➔ Ploumilliau ➔ Locquirec ➔ Guimaëc ➔ Saint-Jean-du-Doigt ➔ Plougasnou (57 km)
* **Weather:** Overcast and showery (6 mm), about **19°C**, with a southerly that was often against us.
* **Log:** Into Finistère. The coast road gave big views from the corniche at Locquirec and the cliffs above Saint-Jean-du-Doigt, where we stopped for a picnic in the late afternoon.
* **Overnight:** Camping Municipal de Pont ar Gler (Plougasnou).

---

### **Wednesday, 10 September 2025**
* **Route:** Plougasnou ➔ Morlaix ➔ Taulé ➔ Saint-Pol-de-Léon (42 km)
* **Weather:** The greyest day: almost no sun, showers (6 mm), about **17°C**.
* **Log:** Around the Bay of Morlaix, under the town's railway viaduct before noon, then through the artichoke and cauliflower fields of the Léon to Saint-Pol-de-Léon and its Kreisker spire by 2 PM.
* **Overnight:** Camping Ar Kleguer (Saint-Pol-de-Léon).

---

### **Thursday, 11 September 2025**
* **Route:** Saint-Pol-de-Léon ➔ Roscoff (11 km) | Roscoff ➔ Plymouth (Ferry) | Plymouth ➔ Bristol (Train)
* **Weather:** A blustery westerly (gusts to 66 km/h), about **18°C**.
* **Log:** A short last ride to the ferry port at Roscoff, arriving at 12:30 PM. On the crossing home there was Mario Kart on board and a sunset over the Channel, then we came into Plymouth Sound around 8:30 PM past the breakwater lighthouse and a couple of navy ships, docking at Millbay before the train back to Bristol. Tour complete!
